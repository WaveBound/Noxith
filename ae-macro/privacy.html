DATA & SECURITY

Privacy Policy

How your information is collected, processed, and secured.

DATA // 01

1. Information We Collect

When purchasing or activating a Noxith Macro license, we collect only the minimal data necessary to fulfill your order and authenticate your software:

• Discord User ID: Used to issue your license key and assign customer roles in the Discord server.
• Email Address (Optional): Provided strictly for payment receipts and order status updates.
• Hardware ID (HWID): A non-reversible cryptographic hash of your system hardware generated upon first activation to bind your key to your device.
• Transaction Metadata: Stripe PaymentIntent / Session identifiers for purchase verification.

DATA // 02

2. Payment Card Security (Stripe)

We do not process, collect, or store your credit card numbers, CVC codes, or expiration dates on our servers.

All payment transactions are handled directly through Stripe, Inc. via PCI-DSS Level 1 certified encrypted connections. Your payment information is subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy.

DATA // 03

3. How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used solely for:

• Generating and verifying license key validity.
• Automatically assigning roles and delivering keys via Discord bot DMs.
• Preventing unauthorized distribution, fraud, and account piracy.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

DATA // 04

4. Third-Party Services

Our infrastructure relies on trusted enterprise cloud platforms:

• Cloudflare Workers & D1: Hosts our API and encrypted authentication database.
• Discord API: Delivers license keys and manages role authorization.
• Stripe: Processes payment authorization and fraud detection.

DATA // 05

5. Data Deletion & Contact

To review our terms of use, please visit our Terms of Service. If you wish to have your database record or associated license data removed, or if you have questions regarding privacy practices, please contact us directly via our Discord Server.
