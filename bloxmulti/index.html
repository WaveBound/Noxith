Dashboard

Your layout,
your way

BloxMulti's four-panel dashboard is made up of fully swappable widgets. Every panel can show a different view — mix and match to build the exact setup you want.

Saved Games
Your bookmarked games with thumbnails and pin support. Click any entry to launch it instantly with your selected account. Sorted by pinned first, then most recently added.
Saved Servers
Private server link codes stored alongside their game. No more digging through messages for links — your crew's servers are always one click away, pinnable and launchable instantly.
Recent Launches
A live feed of the last 10 game launches you made, with timestamps and thumbnails. Great for quickly jumping back into something you were just playing without saving it first.
Running Instances Premium
A real-time monitor for every active Roblox instance on your machine. See live CPU, RAM, and GPU usage per account, and kill any instance with a single click. Available to Premium users.
How to change a widget
Swapping out a panel is instant — no settings menus or restarts needed.
1
Right-click the small label at the top of any dashboard panel (e.g. "SAVED GAMES").
2
A picker appears showing all available widgets. Ones already in use are greyed out.
3
Click the widget you want — the panel swaps instantly and your choice is saved.
Upgrade

Take it further
with Premium

The core app is free forever. Premium unlocks power-user features built for people running serious numbers of accounts.

Groups
Organise your accounts into named groups and launch every account in a group into a game with a single click. Perfect for managing a large roster — add the group to your sidebar just like a regular account, then click to select all and launch.
Per-Account FPS Cap
Set a custom FPS limit for each account independently, overriding the global cap. Throttle background instances down to 30 FPS to save CPU and GPU while keeping your active account silky smooth. Configure it per-account right from the account settings menu.
Window Renaming
When enabled, each Roblox window is automatically renamed to the account's display name as soon as it launches. Instead of five windows all called "Roblox", you see exactly which account is in which window in the taskbar and Task Manager — making it trivial to switch between them.
Running Instances Widget
A live dashboard widget that shows every active Roblox process — matched to the account that launched it — with real-time CPU, RAM, and GPU usage bars updating every few seconds. Kill any instance instantly with one click without hunting through Task Manager.
